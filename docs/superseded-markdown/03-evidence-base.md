# AI-MAP Ethiopia — Evidence Base and Source Register (v0.1)

Every substantive claim gathered during scoping, with its source and an evidence tag.
Tags: `[S]` secondary published source · `[E]` our estimate · `[V]` **unverified, circulating publicly** · `[G]` gap, no evidence found.

> **Verification rule.** Nothing in this register enters the white paper without being checked against the primary document. Items tagged `[V]` must be confirmed against an appropriation, gazette or official release before publication — several widely-repeated figures about Ethiopian AI did not survive scrutiny during scoping.

| Fact | Value | Tag | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| AU Continental AI Strategy | Endorsed by Executive Council July 2024; five focus areas (harness benefits, build capabilities, minimise risks, stimulate investment, foster cooperation); **15 recommendations to member states** | `[S]` | au.int; FPF |

| Africa aggregate | ~38% internet penetration (2024); **<1% of global data centres** | `[S]` | Agbeyangi & Lukose, *Mapping the AI Divide in Africa* |

| **Clearest transferable lesson** | Kenya's separation of *strategy* from a *costed, owned, dated implementation roadmap*. Ethiopia has the former and not the latter | `[E]` | Our synthesis → recommendation §9.1 |
