South Africa Tops African Generative
AI Adoption Rankings in 2026
Microsoft Report
 Friday, 22 May 2026 12:04
South Africa ranked as Africa’s leading user of generative AI tools in the first
quarter of 2026, with a 23.1% adoption rate among people aged 15-64.
Microsoft reported widening disparities between developed and
developing countries, with average GenAI adoption rates of 27.5% and 15.4%
respectively.
The United Arab Emirates remained the global leader in generative AI
usage, with 70.1% of its population using GenAI tools.
South Africa, Namibia, and both Libya and Gabon ranked as Africa’s top users of
generative artificial intelligence tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and
Copilot during the first quarter of 2026, according to a report that Microsoft
published on May 7, 2026.
Titled “Global AI Diffusion - Q1 2026 Trends and Insights,” the report measured
global AI diffusion based on the proportion of people aged 15 to 64 who used a
generative AI solution between January 1 and March 31, 2026. Microsoft based the
study on aggregated and anonymized telemetry data. The company then
adjusted the data to account for differences in operating system and connected
device market shares, internet penetration rates and national populations.
South Africa ranked among the countries where generative AI adoption has
become relatively established. Some 23.1% of the country’s population aged 15 to
64 used the technology during the first quarter of 2026, compared with 19.3%
during the same period a year earlier.
Wide Disparities
With a usage rate of 15.1% during the first three months of 2026, Namibia ranked
second in Africa, ahead of both Libya and Gabon at 15%, followed by Egypt and
Botswana at 14.8%, Senegal at 13.9%, Tunisia at 13.5% and Algeria at 13.2%.
Zambia and Ivory Coast shared 10th place on the continent, with 13.1% of their
populations using generative AI solutions.
(See the full ranking of the 46 African countries surveyed below.)
Globally, the use of generative AI tools increased to an average of 17.8% of the
population aged 15-64 during the first quarter of 2026, compared with 15.1%
during the same period in 2025.
The United Arab Emirates retained its position as the global leader in generative
AI adoption, with 70.1% of its population using the technology. The Gulf monarchy
ranked ahead of Singapore at 63.4%, Norway at 48.6%, Ireland at 48.4% and France
at 47.8%.
Moreover, the report highlighted widening disparities between developed and
developing countries. Developed countries recorded an average generative AI
usage rate of 27.5% during the first quarter of 2026, representing growth of 2.8
percentage points compared with the second half of 2025.
In contrast, developing countries posted an average usage rate of 15.4% during
the first three months of 2026. That figure represented an increase of only 1.3
percentage points compared with the second half of the previous year.
The report said the widening gap reflected systemic challenges across
developing economies, where limited access to reliable electricity, internet
connectivity and digital skills continued to slow generative AI adoption.
“Until these fundamental gaps are addressed, the benefits of this technology will
remain unevenly distributed, risking the further aggravation of existing global
economic inequalities,” the report stated.
